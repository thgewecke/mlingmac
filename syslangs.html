Mac and iPhone/iPad Differences In System Language Support



Users in some countries with both a Mac and iPhone/iPad may notice that their local language is only available in menus and dialogues on the iOS device.


With versions 26 and 27 of MacOS and iOS/iPadOS, there are now significant differences between them regarding system language support.  MacOS lists 33 different languages and iOS lists 46.  The 13 additional languages in  iOS/iPadOS are Bangla, Bulgarian, Gujarati, Kannada, Kazakh, Lithuanian, Malayalam, Marathi, Odia, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu, and Urdu.


Whether the iOS additions will soon be extended to MacOS is unknown.
