Fix for Cantonese Spoken Content Not Working Right



Users of Sequoia may find that Cantonese text to speech does not work in any app except Pages.  Even after setting the System Speech Language to Cantonese and the Voice to Sinji, text gets read as Mandarin instead.


The fix for this is to go to System Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content > System Voice > ⓘ and select Chinese.  In the list of 4 options under Spoken Language click on Cantonese.
